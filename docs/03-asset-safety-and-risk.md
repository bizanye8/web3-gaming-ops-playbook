# 03 · Asset Safety & Risk Control

A matrix multiplies everything — including mistakes. Safety rules are written for the worst day, not the average day.

## Wallet segregation

- **Working wallets** hold only what the current campaign needs. **Settlement wallets** receive proceeds and never sign game contracts. The two never mix.
- One compromised working wallet must be an annoyance, not an obituary: cap per-wallet exposure by rule.

## Approval hygiene

- Grant token approvals narrowly; revoke after campaigns end.
- Treat every new contract interaction as guilty until proven innocent: official link, verified address, small test first — the same probe discipline attackers use, inverted for defense.

## Operational risk

- **Platform risk:** games change rules, delay seasons, or die. Never let one title hold the whole operation's working capital.
- **Market risk:** reward tokens are priced in hype for about a week. The exit rule (doc 02, stage 6) exists because week two is unforgiving.
- **Key-person risk:** if only one person knows the board, the passwords' location, and the plan, the operation has a single point of failure. Document the system so it survives its operator's sick week.

## The standing rule

No single failure — one wallet, one game, one campaign, one bad week — may be allowed to end the operation. Size everything so the worst case is a lesson, not a funeral.
