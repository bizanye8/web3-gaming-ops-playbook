# 04 · Metrics & Review

## The four numbers that matter

Per account, per campaign:

1. **Cost in** — everything: assets, gas, fees, and labor at an honest hourly rate.
2. **Realized out** — settled value only. Unrealized balances go in a separate column and are never added to results.
3. **Net** — (2) − (1). The only scoreboard.
4. **Days** — capital days locked. A ¥10K profit in 10 days and the same profit in 100 days are different businesses.

## Review cadence

- **Weekly:** mark-to-market, stalled accounts, upcoming windows and deadlines.
- **Per campaign:** full post-mortem — planned vs. actual on all four numbers, the three biggest mistakes, the one rule change that follows.
- **Per season:** cohort rankings. Promote what the top cohort did into next season's default playbook; retire what the bottom cohort proves doesn't pay.

## Kill criteria (set before entry, enforced without debate)

- Reward pool estimate falls below the level that covers cohort cost at realistic participation.
- Rules change in a way that strands sunk cost with no path to eligibility.
- The game's own solvency/activity signals break (dead economy, abandoned updates).
- Operator hours required exceed the plan by 2× with no compensating upside.

Killing a campaign early is a profit-protection skill. The accounts you stop feeding are the budget for the next campaign that actually pays.
