# 02 · Airdrop Campaign Workflow

Six stages. Skipping one is how campaigns lose money while "winning" the airdrop.

## 1. Intel

- Read the project's own docs, tokenomics and past-season rules before touching an account.
- Estimate the reward pool against realistic participant counts. A big pool divided by everyone is a small pool.
- Decide the campaign's **kill criteria** now (see doc 04), while you are still objective.

## 2. Eligibility design

- Map every stated requirement (levels, holdings, activity types, snapshots) into a checklist per account.
- Prefer requirements you can verify from the chain or the game's own UI — never from community rumor.

## 3. Execution

- Run cohorts in waves so a rules change or a ban wave never takes the whole matrix at once.
- Keep behavior inside the game's normal patterns: play the game, in human rhythms, at human hours.

## 4. Claim

- Claim windows are deadlines with money attached. Calendar them with redundancy — a missed window is a total loss on that account.
- Verify the claim contract/address from official channels only. Claim season is phishing season.

## 5. Consolidation

- Move proceeds to settlement wallets promptly; campaign wallets are working capital, not storage.
- Record realized value per account the day it settles (doc 04's ledger discipline).

## 6. Exit

- Sell into the post-claim liquidity according to the pre-set rule (ladder out, time-boxed), not into hope.
- Write the campaign post-mortem within a week, while the mistakes are still embarrassing enough to remember.
